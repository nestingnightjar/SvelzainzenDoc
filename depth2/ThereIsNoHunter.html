ESCAPE INFORMATION
Standard Hypnopompia breaching protocol is to be activated at the immediate certainty of (O1)2-03-1's breach.

In its human form, (O1)2-47-1 possesses above-average damage resistance but should be easily suppressed by a team of three trained personnel armed with the standard issue assault rifle. However, due to (O1)2-47-1's electronic interference capabilities, coupled with its ever present fog, it is unlikely that (O1)2-47-1 will be in its human form.



(O1)2-47-1's monster form is both durable and fast. It is recommended to use traps and barricades to slow down the advance of (O1)2-47-1 until the Rapid Response Team arrives. As a last resort, it is known that (O1)2-47-1 flinches upon being shot by a sniper rifle. This may buy enough time to run or hide.

It is a high priority to force (O1)2-47-1 into its human form if it isn't already. In its human form, the Cerathane must be seen by at least 1 person for the duration of the breach. Floodlights or multiple high powered flashlights can help break through the fog. In its monster form, all security camera systems must be disabled and no personnel should engage the target or even look at it while being chased or torn apart.
