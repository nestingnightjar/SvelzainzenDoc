THE SEA OF DREAMS

The Sea of Dreams is a space outside of physical reality, a space that is only accessible while one is asleep. Dreamers are those who have access to the Sea of Dreams, able to enter either naturally or through extensive practice. A Dreamer may find themselves in any of the six layers referred to as depths. Dreamers can not move between depths while dreaming but they may descend lower with each new dream. There are six depths within the Sea of Dreams.

DEPTH I: THE STILL ABYSS

Corresponding Deities: None
All Dreamers start off in the Still Abyss, a vast ocean infinite in depth and size. Despite being infinitely deep, Dreamers stand only ankles deep in the cool water. The Still Abyss is, as its name suggests, completely calm and devoid of any waves. Only the ripples of the Dreamer’s steps disturb the eerily smooth surface. A day-night cycle is observed in the first depth although there is no temperature change between the day and the night. The Dreamer must remember their existence in the physical world and have the will to return to it in order to descend into the second depth. If a Dreamer chooses to stay indefinitely, they may be lost.

DEPTH II: POOLS OF CORRUPTION

Corresponding Deities: Raaniful & Otaltsa
The Pits of Corruption is a dark, stormy ocean completely frozen in ice. Unlike the first depth, temperature is an apparent factor within the second depth. For respite, one may find pools of warm water carved from the icy expanse. The water reflects an aurora despite the sky being perpetually carpeted in dark, heavy clouds. The pools are so warm and so calming that anyone who rests within them may not feel the need to move on at all. If this occurs, the Dreamer may find themselves back at the First Depth next time they dream. To descend into the third depth, a Dreamer must know humility yet have the endurance to obtain victory. Complete submission to the pools or to give up on the cold ice will result in the Dreamer waking up or getting lost.

DEPTH III: CISTERN OF SOUND

Corresponding Deities: Isikoani
The Cistern of Sound consists of islands of floating rock suspended in the sky by a roaring tempest. By way of the tempest winds, Dreamers may fly between the different islands. New to the Sea thus far, figures populate the floating islands. Hazy and shadow-like, the Dreamer must come to them if they hope to continue downwards. A balance between judgement and kindness, humility and endurance must be made in order to descend into the fourth depth. Imbalance leads to a loss of control and thus the Dreamer will either be swept up in the tempest or fall to a higher depth.

DEPTH IV: HILLS OF CLINGING MUD

Corresponding Deities: Kuotas & Matikota
The Hills of Clinging Mud consists of hilly terrain made entirely of warm, deep mud. If a Dreamer stops moving for too long, they risk sinking into the endless mud. Similar to the depths before it, the fourth depth includes manifestations. From the mud appears figures from the Dreamer’s life, usually those whom the Dreamer loves romantically. The figures of mud cling to the body of the Dreamer, showering them with affection. The Dreamer must find a balance between judgement and kindness in order to descend into the fifth depth. Using the figures purely for movement across the mud will antagonize them yet completely indulging in their love is equally as dangerous.

DEPTH IV: OBLIVION AND THE GATE OF DEATH

Corresponding Deities: Latu & Seisi
Oblivion and the Gate of Death consists of a singular grand arch unfathomably vast, standing tall in a lightless void. Still, they see the arch in all its glory. Here, the Dreamer discovers they possess the power to create and to destroy. A Dreamer may spend an eternity here, creating and destroying countless worlds, yet the dream will not end. The arch records every action of every Dreamer that visits oblivion, a narration of countless events carved into its alabaster surface. The Dreamer must recreate the past, a past that the gate has transcribed for the Dreamer. Will the Dreamer be able to relive their past? And will they be able to learn from it in order to build a better future? Those who learn nothing are doomed to an eternity of torture and those who cannot bear to look back may not wake at all.

DEPTH V: THE CENTER

Corresponding Deities: Rin
Absolute nothingness. One may understand themself but can they accept it? Can they accept the reflection of themself as they recall their own life unfolding within the space of their mind? It takes wisdom to overcome adversity. A star burns within, and only when the light is seen can the Dreamer be at peace.

ITERATIONS

Within each depth, one may experience a variety of deviations in the ways in which the depth behaves. Such deviations are called ‘iterations’. Iterations are believed to be distortions brought forth by the unconscious mind. Such distortions take on many shapes, ranging from the addition of foreign objects (trees, doorways, homes, etc.) to the alteration of an entity (i.e. third depth entities being verbally hostile to the Dreamer). The unconscious mind distorts depth localities through:

[0] EMPTINESS: Sense of directionless, unfulfillment, and/or a desire to stop existing.

[1] ISOLATION: Loneliness; a yearning for connection.

[2] FEAR: Horror, terror, aversion, and/or phobia.

[3] OBSESSION: Lust, devotion, and/or desire.

[4] RELIEF: Freedom from a situation.

[5] TRAUMA: The lasting memory of a distressing or disturbing experience.

[6] HATRED: Intense disliking; loathing; unending anger or wrath.

[7] STRANGE: Elements/themes that do not conform to the other categories.

[8] UNCERTAINTY: Doubt, vulnerability, and/or unpredictability.

[9] WORRY: Stress, anxiety, panic, and/or hopelessness.

One emotion/feeling is responsible for the distortion of the depth into a personalized iteration that follows the general structure of what each of the eight mean and represent. In most cases, each Dreamer experiences their own unique iteration even if they are both present at the same depth. In some cases, multiple dreamers may inhabit the same iteration, heightening the complexity of the dream.
