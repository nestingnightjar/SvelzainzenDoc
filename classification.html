DREAM CLASSIFICATION GUIDE

Svelzainzen IDs are composed of three distinct parts: the Dream Origin, Dreamer Origin, and REM. Threshold Consciousness is a separate metric. An example of a full ID would be (E1)4-67-1.

DREAM ORIGIN

The dream origin defines the most probable source of a dream. The Dream Origin Designator (DOD) starts with the first letter shown in brackets followed by the Sea Depth.

The origin designator is composed of a dream attribute (letter) and number. There are 10 attribute identifiers:

[0] (E)MPTINESS: Sense of directionless, unfulfillment, and/or a desire to stop existing.

[1] (I)SOLATION: Loneliness; a yearning for connection.

[2] (F)EAR: Horror, terror, aversion, and/or phobia.

[3] (O)BSESSION: Lust, devotion, and/or desire.

[4] (R)ELIEF: Freedom from a situation.

[5] (T)RAUMA: The lasting memory of a distressing or disturbing experience.

[6] (H)ATRED: Intense disliking; loathing; unending anger or wrath.

[7] (S)TRANGE: Elements/themes that do not conform to the other categories.

[8] (U)NCERTAINTY: Doubt, vulnerability, and/or unpredictability.

[9] (W)ORRY: Stress, anxiety, panic, and/or hopelessness.

An iteration number represents a unique dream iteration. If multiple Svelzainzen share the same iteration number, then each individual Dreamer has gotten lost in the same dream iteration within the same dream layer. An iteration number of 0 means no iteration.

There are six layers of Sea Depth:

1. Still Abyss (Top)
2. Pools of Corruption
3. Cistern of Sound
4. Plane of Clinging Mud
5. Oblivion & the Gate of Death
6. The Center of You (Bottom)

For more information regarding Sea Depths, see the subpage titled Sea Depths. Together, iteration and Sea Depth make up the first section of a Dreamer ID: (AttributeID+Iteration#)SeaDepth.

DREAM CHARACTERIZATION

The Dreamer Characterization Number (DCN) is made up of two digits. The left digit is the primary origin, and the right digit is the secondary origin. There are 10 numbers corresponding to unique descriptive attributes:

[0] HATEFUL: The Dreamer feels an overwhelming sense of anger/hate.

[1] DESIRING: The Dreamer feels an overwhelming desire to spread a part of themself, such as genetics or ideals.

[2] LOST: The Dreamer feels a sense of directionlessness and submits to the will of others.

[3] YEARNING: The Dreamer is constantly searching for and trying to acquire something that is often out of reach or will never satisfy them.

[4] MELONCHOLIC: The Dreamer feels an overwhelming sense of sadness.

[5] PRIDEFUL: The Dreamer is overconfident in their abilities and/or feels a sense of infallibility.

[6] ENVIOUS: The Dreamer feels inadequate compared to others around them, in addition to feelings of resentment.

[7] FEARFUL: The Dreamer feels an overwhelming sense of fear, anxiety, and/or paranoia.

[8] MANIC: The Dreamer is in a frenzied and unstable state while also being extremely excitable. They may or may not be disconnected from reality.

[9] PURE: The Dreamer feels indifference towards life and those around them. Related to apathy.


THRESHOLD CONSCIOUSNESS & REM

Threshold Consciousness is a determinant of a Cerathane’s threat to the lives of staff.
There are 5 categories of Threshold Consciousnesses, from least to most dangerous:
Lison (Sleep)
Lisokoya (Hypnagogia)
Alom (Dream)
Siut (Nightmare)
Plakia (Plegia)

REM is the last digit of a Dreamer ID. It represents the number of Dreamers that make up a Svelzainzen. The three REM classifications are:
1. Single
2. Couple
3. Many
