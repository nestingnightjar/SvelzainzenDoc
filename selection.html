DEPTH I: THE STILL ABYSS

The infinite ocean’s surface hugs closely to my ankles. Look at this beautiful sunset, protesting against freedom.
Though, what’s so wrong with comfort anyways?

DEPTH II: POOLS OF CORRUPTION

Stepping onto the cragged ice, the cold is so painfully unbearable.
Yet, I am not permitted to remain within the embrace of the warm pools. See how cruel this world is?

DEPTH III: CISTERN OF SOUND

A current of wind takes me to an island, one surrounded not with water but with air.
There are people here; maybe they can help me? Or maybe they’ll hurt me…

DEPTH IV: HILLS OF CLINGING MUD

A hand of mud lunges for my ankles. Looking down, I see someone whom I love.
Instinctual fear grips me before they do as I jump away.
Should I be afraid? I take a step forward; I know it’s not really them, but am I not deserving of even a bit of affection after all I’ve been through?

DEPTH V: OBLIVION AND THE GATE OF DEATH

An arch stands before me, one so grand it could be mistaken for a mountain. Etched across its surface is the story of my being.
But why dwell on the past when I can create whatever future I want here?
