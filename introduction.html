FORWARD

This document is part of the onboarding process for new employees working for the Ministry of Svelzainzen Containment and Research (MSCR). This document includes a list of and information regarding the ministry’s sub-departments and their associated Overseer.

Each Overseer had provided additional information regarding what it’s like working in their respective sub-department and what they value in their employees. It is highly recommended that the reader does not skip the ‘Overseer Notes’. If the reader has any sub-department related questions, direct them to the sub-department contact and not the Overseer’s contact unless explicitly stated otherwise. Other questions should be sent to the Ministry’s help desk at mscr.helpdesk@email.yr.

By the end of the week, all onboarding employees will be given a quiz to test their knowledge. This quiz will be given out via email; however, should an employee wish to take the quiz at an MSCR site, contact the specific site’s help desk and a space will be provided.

INTRODUCTION

The Ministry of Svelzainzen Containment and Research is responsible for keeping the people of Yiryor safe from Svelzainzens —entities, locations, events, or even abstract concepts which defy conventional thinking and/or the laws of science. Due to the anomalous nature of Svelzainzens, they pose a serious threat to Yiryoran livelihood. The MSCR is, first and foremost, a ministry of security. However, Svelzainzens symbolize a new frontier of science and should be studied rather than destroyed. Researching Svelzainzens is an inherently risky venture. As such, special procedures are in place to maximize the safety and security of the ministry’s employees. It is imperative that you, a new employee, familiarize yourself with the MSCR’s sub-departments and procedures so that you and your colleagues remain safe around dangerous phenomena.
