ESCAPE INFORMATION
When replication work fails, (W1)1-24-1 breaches containment by replicating itself between two random rooms within the confines of the MSCR Headquarters. Replicated skies do not deviate in any way from the original other than the dimensions of the manifested room.



Standard Hypnagogia breaching protocol is not necessary for (W1)1-24-1. Thus, employees are to follow these steps when encountering a replicated sky:
1. Report the location of the Svelzainzen to internal security;
2. Attach a note (or any other method of identification) on the affected door;
3. Find an appropriate detour and submit an e-memo of the incident (use).

In the event that one encounters a replicated sky during an emergency, jumping into the sky may be the only action to keep oneself safe. Avoid the surrounding walls at all times while free falling and wait for help to arrive. mportantly, ensure someone knows you’re free falling so that re-containment can occur after your rescue.
