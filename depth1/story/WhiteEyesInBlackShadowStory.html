WHITE EYES IN BLACK SHADOW
“Stop looking, why won’t everyone avert their eyes?! Let me blend into the dark and remain hidden forevermore.”

(R1)1-26-3, or “White Eyes in Black Shadow”, is an electromagnetic radiative phenomenon resembling that of a permeating shadow. (R1)1-26-3’s range varies sporadically, but always covers around half of the room which it is confined within. Stepping into (R1)1-26-3 poses no physical or psychological threat in itself, and behaves with the same characteristics of a shadow. Within (R1)1-26-3, three animal-like beings classified as “Residents” patrol regularly, always within the darkness of (R1)1-26-3. If one or more Residents is lost for whatever reason, another Resident will spontaneously appear within (R1)1-26-3 after around 24 hours.

The Residents of (R1)1-26-3 measure around 2 m long and 1.5 m tall, weighing about 100 lbs. Residents are able to traverse through any physical space, but exhibit a preference to shadowed locations (whether it be naturally forming or (R1)1-26-3 itself). Although Residents exhibit caution when in proximity with Z-F, they often exhibit Staring. The Residents characteristically possess blindly pale eyes around the diameter of a dinner plate, and tend to lock their vision to Z-Fs visible to them (coined as Staring). During the event of a Staring, Z-Fs (typically of lower ranks) report experiencing psychological symptoms such as hallucinations, disorientating thinking, delusions, and difficulties breathing. Symptoms appear to intensify multiplicatively depending on the number of Residents Staring at an individual.

Note that replication work is only available when the maximum number of Residents are present in (R1)1-26-3.
