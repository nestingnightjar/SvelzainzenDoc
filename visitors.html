THE VISITOR PHENOMENON

It has become increasingly clear that there exists entities that exist independently of a dreamer’s dream. The Ego Fragmentation Theory, developed by Research Overseer Satariel, offers an explanation to these “Visitors”. The theory states that a fragment of the lost dreamer’s consciousness may, at any time, become lucid; that is, a rogue breakaway that had regained its ability to reason and think. This stands in contrast to the lost dreamer, whose mental state is effectively stuck in a stasis determined by the dream that they had gotten lost within.

Another key difference between the Visitor and the lost dreamer is behavior; by understanding the dream, one can understand the Svelzainzen. Visitors, on the other hand, are not bound by the dream. As such, they are inherently unpredictable. Moreover, the Visitor is capable of (and may frequently do) spontaneous dream jumping; meaning, a Visitor can appear in any dream, including replication work.

In the event that you encounter a Visitor during routine replication work, stop what you’re doing and follow these steps:

1. Activate the replication emergency alarm;
2. Describe the appearance of the Visitor and what it is doing;
3. A member of the Research Department (or Archival) will attempt to identify the Visitor (see the list below) and supply you with information and recommendations.
4. Newer Z-F operatives are recommended to follow their word, while senior Z-F operatives should follow their own judgement;
5. If the Visitor is undocumented, state your observations for the researcher. The researcher will present you with questions to ask the Visitor;
6. Resume replication at the order of the researcher. If the Visitor interferes with replication or shows signs of hostility, request immediate ejection.
